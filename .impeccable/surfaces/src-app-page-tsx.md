# Home (portfolio)

Scope: `/` single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport).

Audience: hiring teams and small-business clients. Action: open the Aroma case study, then email or GitHub.
Proof: Aroma booking platform (live), archive of shipped/practice projects with live screenshots, real GitHub contribution calendar.
Constraints: no invented claims, metrics or testimonials; portrait photo pending; email pending.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio that smolders: the developer's work surfaces out of a molten field rather than sitting in a card grid. Refuses the dark-dev-portfolio default of a split hero with an avatar, a skills badge cloud and three equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ember ground (#0c0504), molten reds (#ff3a1c, #c4150b, #5c0905) and a rare amber highlight (#ff9a4a) moving as a WebGL lava-lamp field under animated film grain. Bone text (#f4e6dc). Unbounded for display (wide, Cyrillic-capable), Golos Text for body, JetBrains Mono only for dates, slot times and data. Hairline ember rules and rings, 2px corners, no cards, no soft glows.

STORY: Visitor sees the name and the real Aroma site rising out of the heat, understands this person ships full-stack products with payments and booking logic, scrolls through the case study, scans the archive and contribution heat map, then writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed lava canvas. Top: monogram left, four links, email action right. Bottom: "Ankhbayar" at viewport-wide display scale with "Munkhbaatar", the role line and the Cyrillic name beneath. Right, from about a fifth of the way down: the Aroma screenshot under heat haze, dissolving into the lava under the name (masked fade instead of a hard fold crop), captioned as the featured work. Primary action: "See the Aroma case study" plus email link in nav.

FORM: Pinned by the user's template brief ("Creative Director": molten reds drift like a lava lamp beneath film grain while case studies glide past in a slow heat haze), so it overrides the roll; the roll ran afterwards only to record a reference and its assignment was not adopted. seed:d4e0d598d5402a36
Signature interaction: case-study screenshots glide on scroll and their heat-haze displacement cools to sharp as they reach the viewport centre; archive rows reveal a hazed preview that follows the cursor; a slot-grid demonstration of Aroma's booking logic.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
